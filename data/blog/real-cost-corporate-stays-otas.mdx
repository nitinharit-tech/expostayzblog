Every time your team books a hotel on an OTA, the hotel pays 15-25% commission on that room night. That money does not disappear. It gets baked into the rate your company pays, and it buys you exactly one thing: a transaction.

## What the commission really costs

A room that costs the hotel nothing extra to sell direct still carries the OTA markup when it flows through a third party. For a company putting up even 50 room nights a month, that is the difference between a negotiated corporate rate and whatever the algorithm shows that day.

Worse, the guest relationship stays with the platform. The hotel cannot recognize your traveler, cannot upgrade them, cannot learn their preferences. Your most frequent traveler is a stranger every single stay.

## What companies actually need

Corporate stays are not leisure bookings. The requirements are different:

- **Consolidated billing** instead of fifty separate card charges
- **Negotiated rates** that reflect real volume, not dynamic pricing
- **Duty of care**: knowing where your people are, every night
- **Policy compliance** built into the booking flow, not enforced after

None of this is what OTAs are built for. They are built to sell one room to one person, once.

## The fix is boring, and it works

Book direct with hotels or through a corporate stay partner. Negotiate rates against your actual volume. Put every stay on one invoice. It is less exciting than a flash sale, and it saves real money every single month.
